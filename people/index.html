Magdalena Fuentes

Assistant Professor
Music Technology and Integrated Design & Media
New York University

Magdalena Fuentes is Assistant Professor of Interactive Design, Music and Sound at New York University. She is appointed in Music Technology at the Steinhardt School of Culture, Education, and Human Development and in Integrated Design & Media at the Tandon School of Engineering. She is core faculty in the Music and Audio Research Laboratory (MARL) and affiliated faculty in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Tandon.

Her research develops signal processing and machine learning methods for machine listening: how computers interpret music and environmental sound, how sound connects with vision and language, and how those models can be made more robust, interpretable, and reliable. Her work spans music information retrieval, environmental sound understanding, multimodal analysis and synthesis, and applications in accessibility and health. Her work has been supported by the National Science Foundation, the National Institutes of Health, Google, Adobe, and NYU.

She received her Ph.D. from Université Paris-Saclay, France, in 2019. She studied electrical engineering at Universidad de la República, Uruguay, and completed her B.Eng. thesis, “Procesamiento multimodal de señales en la interpretación de música” in 2015. That thesis received the best undergraduate thesis award from the National Academy of Engineering of Uruguay. She received the NYU Provost’s Postdoctoral Fellowship (2019), the Google Award for Inclusion Research (2024), and the NYU Steinhardt Gabriel Carras Research Award for Promising Young Scholars (2025).

She co-organizes the Latin American Music Information Retrieval Workshop (LAMIR) and, since 2021, has helped organizing the Latin American BISH Bash. She has served as Scientific Program Chair of ISMIR 2025, Grants Chair of ISMIR 2026, Technical Program Chair of the DCASE Workshop (2021, 2023, 2025), and member of the IEEE Audio and Acoustic Signal Processing Technical Committee (2021–2023 and 2023–2026). She has been Area Chair at ICASSP (2022–2026), Diversity Chair at WASPAA 2023, Awards Chair of the DCASE Workshop (2021, 2023, 2025), meta-reviewer at ISMIR (2022, 2026), Special Session Chair at ISMIR 2020, and a member of the local committee at ISMIR 2018. She coordinated the DCASE 2020 Urban Sound Tagging task, co-organized the 2019 workshop on Computational Models for Rhythm and Expressiveness, and is guest editor of the TISMIR special collection on open music data (2026) and co-organizer of Dagstuhl Seminar 26272, Open Music Data for Music Processing Research (2026). She mentors through IEEE SigMA, IEEE ME-UYR (2022, 2024), WiMIR, LXAI NeurIPS (2023), and NYU’s ARISE and REU programs, and reviews for ISMIR, ICASSP, DCASE, TASLP, PLOS ONE, JOSS, TISMIR, and AAAI.
